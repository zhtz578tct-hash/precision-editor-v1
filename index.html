Why results were bad before

• The LoRA Studio engine stacks a full-strength style LoRA (weight 1.0) on a 4-step distilled transformer — far too few steps for a LoRA to resolve. That produces repainted faces, neon colour shifts and garbled pseudo-text.
• Its own default LoRA is Photo-to-Anime, so any photo run through it gets stylised by default.
• Faithful (official Qwen, 50 steps, no LoRA) and Restore (28 steps, restoration LoRAs) are the right engines for keeping a photo looking like the photo.
• Seed is now randomized by default. Before, every run reused seed 0 and produced the identical bad sample.

Notes

• Free Spaces sleep when idle — first request can take 30–60 s.
• Output resolution is derived from your first uploaded image; small inputs give small outputs.
• Save PNG may be blocked inside a sandboxed preview iframe — works when opened normally.
• The LoRA Studio engine applies a content filter; blocked prompts return a message.
