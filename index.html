Notes & limits

• Free Spaces sleep when idle; the first request can take 30–60 s to wake and will queue under load.
• Images are sent to the Space as base64 JSON, so nothing is uploaded to a third party of your own.
• Save PNG may be blocked when this page runs inside a sandboxed preview iframe — open the file directly in your browser (or host it) and it works normally.
• The Space applies a content filter (NCII guard); blocked prompts return a message instead of an image.
• Some Spaces expose a plain /predict endpoint instead of named ones — use the Custom Space tab for those.
